Sights GPS

Road-trip navigation that shows you what's worth stopping for

Plan a drive anywhere in the United States, Canada or Mexico and Sights GPS lists the sights along your route — from must-see landmarks and national-park viewpoints to roadside overlooks and quirky roadside attractions — ranked so the best ones come first.

  • Thousands of North American sights, stored on your phone — no account needed
  • Three tiers: Must See, Interesting, and Roadside views
  • Add stops to your route with one tap and get turn-by-turn directions
  • Visit Mode plans a day in a city, with the trains and buses to take between sights
  • Rate the sights you visit and keep private notes
